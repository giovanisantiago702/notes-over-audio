# Privacy

Notes Over Audio keeps what you mark and write on your own computer, and sends it nowhere.

- **What it stores.** Your Highlights and Notes, with the id, title and channel of each video they are on, and one setting. They are kept inside Chrome, in the profile the extension is installed in.
- **What it sends.** Nothing of yours. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising.
- **What it asks the network for.** The captions of the video you are watching. It asks them of YouTube, from the YouTube page you are on.
- **What it reads.** Pages on `www.youtube.com`, and no other site. There it reads which video is playing, the video's captions and its time, and the keys H, N and U. While you write a Note, it also reads what you type, to put it in the Note.
- **How to delete it all.** Remove the extension. Everything it stored goes with it.

Questions are welcome as an issue at https://github.com/giovanisantiago702/notes-over-audio/issues.
